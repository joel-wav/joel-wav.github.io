03.projects

01 /

3D Coverage Planning with Deep RL

Camera viewpoint selection for automated 3D surface inspection with a UR5e arm, formulated as an MDP over candidate viewpoints sampled between concentric spheres around the object. A shaped reward favors new coverage and penalizes overlap and revisits; a PPO agent learns the policy and is fine-tuned on new objects.

> 99% surface coverage with as few as 11 viewpoints
